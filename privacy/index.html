ikc Waitlist Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

Who we are

The ikc waitlist is run by 深圳市可宸科技有限公司 (Shenzhen Kechen Technology Co., Ltd.), the company building ikc, a Shopify app for wholesale credit limits and Net 30 terms. Questions about this policy: ykunping@gmail.com.

What we collect

We only collect what you enter in the waitlist form:

  • Your email address.
  • Your store name.
  • What you use today to manage credit limits and payments.
  • Your monthly wholesale order volume (optional).
  • What you would pay per month.
  • Whether you are interested in prepaying for the founding early-bird plan.

How we use it

We use this information for three things only:

  • To email you an invitation to a short interview about how you handle wholesale credit and payments.
  • To notify you when ikc launches.
  • To evaluate demand and decide what to build.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not share your information with third parties for their marketing.

Where it is stored

The waitlist form runs on Google Forms, and your answers are kept in Google Sheets. Both services are provided by Google LLC, and your answers are stored on Google's servers. We delete waitlist information within 12 months after the validation period ends. If you ask us to delete it sooner, we will delete it then.

Deleting your information

You can ask us to delete your information at any time. Email ykunping@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, and we will remove you from the waitlist.

Changes and contact

If we change how we use waitlist information, we will update this page and its date. Contact: 深圳市可宸科技有限公司, ykunping@gmail.com.
