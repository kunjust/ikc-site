FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

Does ikc process payments?

No. ikc doesn't collect or process payments. You record payments you've already received offline, such as bank transfers.

Does ikc score my buyers' credit?

No. ikc doesn't do credit scoring. You set each buyer's credit limit and Net 30 terms yourself.

What happens when a buyer is overdue or over their limit?

Checkout is blocked, and the buyer sees a prompt to contact the finance team. When you record a payment and available credit is restored, checkout unblocks.

My wholesale buyers are tagged customers on my regular store. Does ikc work?

Yes. Tag-based wholesale stores can still set credit limits and block checkout with ikc.
